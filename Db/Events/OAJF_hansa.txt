# Ground rules:

# A Hanseatic kontor is worth 5 tax value to its province and 5 tax value to
#the province of Mecklenburg.  A moved kontor does not damage Mecklenburg tax
#value.  A closed kontor does damage Mecklenburg tax value.  A move costs -25
#trade and a close costs -50 trade.  Reopening after a close gains back 25
#trade.

# Murder of merchants is worth -25 trade value and a loss of 1 merchant.

# Successful imposition or increase of tariffs is Mercantilism +1.  Opening the
#Sund or lowering tariffs is Mercantilism -1.  Tariff events depend on
#Mercantilism >= 5.

# Loss of kontor control is worth -2 tax value and -50 trade.

# Loss of a Hansa member or is worth -5 tax value to Mecklenburg and the Hansa
#member and -100 trade.  Readmission of the member is worth +5 tax value to both
#and +50 trade.

# The Hansa events are 187xxx.  The Hansa leaders and monarchs are 187xx.
