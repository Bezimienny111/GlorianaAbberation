Fearing for his safety on her flight, Giovanna chose to leave her young heir, Charles Martel, born posthumously of her husband,Andre, in the capital. Recognizing his importance to the Angevins of Hungary, Giovanna trusted that his uncle, Louis, would protect the child. Her trust was to prove unfounded as the young princeling was to die while being transported back to Hungary."
