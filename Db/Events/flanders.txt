#(1345) Van Artevelde's Assassination

      }

      country = FLA
      name = "Van Artevelde's Assassination"
      desc = "Despite all hopes, the English alliance didn't ameliorate the economic situation in Flanders. Edward III, having quickly understood the tumultuous situation was reluctant to support permanently a rebel government and eventually recalled his troops after several years of immobilisation. In an act of desperation, Artevelde offered the county to Edward's son, contravening all feudal laws as Louis de Nevers, the legitimate count, was alive and no worse for the wear. The King was intrigued with the offer and came personnaly in Flanders to negotiate with the rebel leader, but Flemish popular opinion changed. Van Artevelde was assassinated on July 17, 1345 at Ghent after a popular rebellion. Viewing the situation as a quagmire Edward III went home to England."
