# Starter Four Alternate Destinies: Concept & Architecture Plan
**Gloriana 1337 Mod — Modules 8 to 11**

This plan outlines the design, decisions, event chains, and ideological branches for the final four starter nations:
1. **Mali Empire (MAL)** — Trans-Saharan Reconquista of Iberia vs Transatlantic Empire of Abu Bakr II
2. **Kingdom of Great Zimbabwe (ZIM)** — Pan-African Unification from the Swahili Coast to Morocco
3. **Kingdom of Georgia (GEO)** — Caucasian Christian Empire & Shield against Sunni and Shiite Hegemony
4. **Blue & Golden Horde (BHR / WHR -> STE)** — Halting the Collapse and Rebuilding the Empire of Genghis Khan

---

## 1. Mali Empire (MAL) — The Golden Lion of the Sahel

### Starting Situation (1337)
* **Capital**: Niani / Timbuktu
* **Ruler**: End of the reign of Mansa Musa / accession of Maghan I. The richest empire on earth, controlling the goldfields of Bambuk and Bure and the salt routes of Taghaza.
* **Challenges**: Mossi raids, Songhai independence sentiments, and eventual Saharan isolation.

### Core Strategic Choices
#### Branch A: The Trans-Saharan Caliphate & Reconquista of Iberia
* **Concept**: Emulating the Almoravids and Almohads, but under a Sub-Saharan African Mandinka dynasty.
* **Progression**:
  1. *Subjugation of the Western Sahara*: Annexing the Tuareg oasis networks and establishing imperial garrisons across Mauritania.
  2. *March across the Atlas*: Toppling the Marinid Sultanate in Fez and Morocco, securing Atlantic and Mediterranean ports (Tangiers, Ceuta).
  3. *Crossing the Straits (Tarifa & Gibraltar)*: Military intervention in Andalusia.
  4. *The African Reconquista*: Conquering Granada, Seville, Cordoba, and Toledo. Proclaiming the **Mandinka-Andalusian Sultanate** with dual capitals at Timbuktu and Cordoba.
* **Key Decisions & Events**:
  - `Edict of the Golden Dinar`: Flooding Mediterranean markets with high-purity gold currency.
  - `Crossroads of Gibraltar`: Reverse reconquista against Castile and Portugal.
  - `University of Sankore-Cordoba`: Fusion of Andalusian and West African Islamic science.

#### Branch B: The Ocean of Mansa Abu Bakr II (Transatlantic Empire)
* **Concept**: Fulfilling the legendary voyage of Mansa Abu Bakr II (who set sail with 2,000 vessels in 1311 into the Atlantic).
* **Progression**:
  1. *Atlantic Shipyards of Senegambia*: Constructing high-seas ocean fleets in the estuaries of Gambia and Senegal.
  2. *The Equatorial Crossing*: Sailing the Canary and Guinea currents across the narrow Atlantic to the Brazilian coast (Pernambuco / Maranhao).
  3. *New Mali (Mali Kura)*: Establishing coastal trading colonies, sugarcane plantations, and gold extraction in South America and the Caribbean.
  4. *Maritime Trade Monopoly*: Direct export of West African textiles, gold, and tropical produce to the New World without European middlemen.

## 2. Empire of Great Zimbabwe (ZIM) — Pan-African Hegemony

### Starting Situation (1337)
* **Capital**: Great Zimbabwe (Dzimbahwe)
* **Status**: Zenith of dry-stone monumental architecture (Great Enclosure), thriving gold and ivory trade via Sofala to the Indian Ocean.
* **Challenges**: Continental isolation, lack of ocean-going navy, and vast uncharted territory to the north and west.

### Core Strategic Path: The Unification of Africa
#### Phase 1: Conquest of the Swahili Coast & Indian Ocean Dominance
* Subjugating the merchant city-states of Sofala, Kilwa, Zanzibar, and Malindi.
* Building an ocean-going fleet of dhows and galleons to control the monsoon trade routes to Oman, Gujarat, and Malabar.

#### Phase 2: Trans-Zambezi & Great Lakes Expansion
* Expanding across the Zambezi into the Kingdom of Mutapa and Maravi.
* Pushing into the Great Rift Valley, uniting the interior Bantu kingdoms and controlling copper and iron trade.

#### Phase 3: The Trans-Continental March to Mali and Morocco
* Piercing the Congo basin and crossing the Sahel corridor (Chad Basin, Kanem-Bornu, Hausaland).
* Reaching the gold lands of Mali and marching across the Sahara into Morocco, uniting Sub-Saharan, East, and North Africa under the **Universal Empire of Zimbabwe**.

#### Cultural & Religious Flavor:
* **The Cult of Mwari**: Supreme creator deity worshipped from the Matobo Hills.
* **Megalithic Stone Architecture**: Spreading dry-stone fortress technology (`Dzimbahwe`) to every conquered province.
* **The Zimbabwe Bird (Hungwe)**: Sovereign symbol of the divinely sanctioned Pan-African monarchy.

## 3. Kingdom of Georgia (GEO) — The Caucasian Shield of the Cross

### Starting Situation (1337)
* **Capital**: Tbilisi
* **Ruler**: George V the Brilliant (Giorgi V Brtskinvale), who successfully reunited Kartli, Kakheti, and Imereti, driving out Mongol overlords and reforming Georgian law (*Dzeglisdeba*).
* **Challenges**: Trapped between the collapse of the Mongol Ilkhanate, predatory nomadic confederations (Aq Qoyunlu, Kara Qoyunlu), rising Ottoman power to the west, and emerging Persian Shiite empires to the east.

### Core Strategic Path: The Caucasian Christian Empire
#### Phase 1: Consolidation of the Golden Age of George V
* Eliminating regional feudal separatism (Eristavis) and centralizing the crown.
* Fortifying the Darial Gorge ("Caspian Gates") against steppe nomadic incursions.

#### Phase 2: The Double Shield (Anti-Sunni & Anti-Shiite Resistance)
* Repelling Ottoman expansion across northern Anatolia and the Pontus.
* Defeating Turkmen confederations and preventing Iranian Shiite subjugation (Safavids/Tabriz).
* Championing the liberation of Armenian Christians and restoring the ancient Bagratuni crown under Georgian personal union.

#### Phase 3: The Caucasian Empire & Pontic Hegemony
* Unifying the entire Caucasus: Georgia, Armenia, Shirvan, Circassia, and Dagestan.
* Restoring the Empire of Trebizond as an imperial protectorate, securing the Black Sea coast.
* Proclaiming the **Greater Caucasian Empire of the Cross** with capitals at Tbilisi and Ani.

## 4. Blue & Golden Horde (BHR / WHR -> STE) — Restoration of Genghis Khan's Empire

### Starting Situation (1337)
* **Tag**: Blue Horde (`BHR`) in the West (Batu's Ulus, Volga, Sarai, Crimea, Ukraine) and White Horde (`WHR`) in the East (Orda's Ulus, Kazakh steppes).
* **Ruler of BHR**: Uzbek Khan (1313–1341) — the Golden Horde at its absolute territorial and economic height.
* **Crisis to Prevent**: The Black Death (1346) and the catastrophic "Great Troubles" (1359–1381) which historically fractured the Horde into Kazan, Astrakhan, Crimea, and Sibir, enabling the rise of Muscovy.

### Core Strategic Path: Halting the Collapse & The Great Reunion
#### Phase 1: Quelling Dynastic Turmoil & Administrative Reforms
* Enacting the *Yassa of Uzbek*: Streamlining succession, eliminating internal pretenders, and fortifying Sarai al-Jadid on the Volga.
* Unifying the Blue Horde (`BHR`) and White Horde (`WHR`) into the unified **Great Golden Horde (`STE`)**.

#### Phase 2: Permanent Yoke over the Rus
* Crushing the ambitions of Moscow, Vladimir, and Novgorod.
* Ending the system of indirect tribute collecting (Baskaks) and establishing direct administrative governors, making Russian principalities permanent imperial provinces of the Horde.

#### Phase 3: The Grand Mongolian Restoration (Pax Mongolica Redux)
* Sweeping south into Persia to claim the fractured lands of the Ilkhanate.
* Sweeping east to subjugate the Chagatai Khanate and reclaim Karakorum.
* Confronting Ming China for the restoration of the **Great Yuan / Mongol World Empire**.

#### Religious & Cultural Paths:
* **The Islamic Steppe Caliphate**: Uzbek Khan's legacy — declaring Sarai the capital of Eurasian Islamic trade and cavalry warfare.
* **The Tengrist / Yassa Imperial Path**: Reverting to the ancient laws of Genghis Khan and freedom of worship, uniting nomadic warriors under the Eternal Blue Sky.
